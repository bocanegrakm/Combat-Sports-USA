Privacy Policy

Effective November 7, 2026

The short version.

  • We only collect what you type into our free-class form.
  • We use it to call or email you about classes. We never sell it.
  • Our website doesn't use tracking or advertising cookies.
  • Email us any time to see or delete your information.

Who we are

Combat Sports USA is a family martial arts gym at 4416 Portsmouth Blvd, Ste A, Chesapeake, VA 23321. This policy explains how we handle information collected through combatsportsusa.com. "We" and "us" mean Combat Sports USA. "You" means anyone who visits the site.

What we collect

When you fill out the "Request a free class" form, we collect:

  • Your name, phone number and email address
  • The program you're interested in and your preferred day
  • The student's name and age, if you're booking for a child
  • Anything you write in the notes box

We don't ask for payment details, Social Security numbers or medical records on this website. Please don't include them in the notes box.

Like most websites, our web host may automatically log basic technical details when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type and the pages you viewed. These logs help keep the site running and secure.

How we use it

  • To contact you about your free class and help you pick a program and time
  • To answer questions you send us
  • To keep records of who has booked a free class

We won't add you to a marketing list unless you ask to be added.

Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent your information. We share it only with services that help us run the website:

  • FormSubmit (formsubmit.co) receives your form entry and emails it to info@combatsportsusa.com. See formsubmit.co for how they handle data.
  • Our email provider stores the messages we receive.
  • Google Fonts serves the typefaces on this site. Your browser requests them from Google, which can see your IP address.
  • Our web host stores the site and its server logs.

We may also share information if the law requires it, or to protect the safety of our members and staff.

Children's privacy

Many of our students are children, but this website is meant for parents and guardians. Please have a parent or guardian fill out the form for any student under 18. We don't knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us information, email us and we'll delete it.

Cookies

Our website doesn't set tracking, analytics or advertising cookies. If we add analytics later, we'll update this policy first.

Links to other sites

Our site links to Instagram, Facebook and Google Maps. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and this policy doesn't cover them.

How long we keep it

We keep free-class requests for up to two years so we can follow up and keep accurate records. After that, we delete them unless you've become a member, in which case your membership records take over.

Your choices

You can ask us to show you, correct or delete the information you've sent us. Email info@combatsportsusa.com or call (757) 712-4365, and we'll respond within 30 days.

Keeping it safe

We limit access to form requests to our staff and use reputable services to deliver and store them. No method of sending information over the internet is completely secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version here and update the effective date at the top.
