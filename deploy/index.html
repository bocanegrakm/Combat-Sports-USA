Questions

Before your first class

What do I need to bring or buy?

Shin guards, boxing gloves and black workout shorts once you start. As you advance, you'll want your own headgear and body shield as well.

How big are the classes?

We keep classes to around 20 students per session, so sign up soon.

Will I get hit?

You don't have to. Striking is part of the workout, but you never have to spar if you'd rather not.

Will my medical condition or age prevent me from training?

Check with your doctor first; only you know your limits. Our members train with all kinds of health conditions and set new personal bests every week.
